Bacon Pancakes

Bacon Pancakes are a delicious breakfast option that combines the savory flavors of bacon with the comforting texture of pancakes. Here's a simple recipe to make them at home:

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 4 slices cooked bacon, crumbled

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and add the egg, milk, and vegetable oil. Mix until smooth.
  3. Fold in the crumbled bacon.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, about 2 to 3 minutes.
  7. Flip and cook until golden brown, about 2 to 3 minutes more.
  8. Serve hot with your favorite pancake toppings, such as maple syrup, butter, or fresh fruit.
