Carrot Halwa

Carrot Halwa is a delicious and healthy dessert made with grated carrots, milk, sugar, and ghee. It is a popular Indian sweet dish that is often served during festivals and special occasions. The carrots give the halwa a bea utiful orange color and a sweet, earthy flavor. The milk and ghee make it creamy and rich, while the sugar adds a touch of sweetness. Carrot Halwa is a great way to enjoy carrots in a sweet and comforting dessert.

Ingredients:

  • 250 grams grated carrots
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup ghee
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon saffron strands soaked in 1 tablespoon milk

Instructions:

  1. In a heavy-bottomed pan, heat ghee over medium heat. Add grated carrots and sauté until they turn soft and golden brown. This may take about 10-15 minutes.
  2. Add milk and sugar to the pan. Stir well to combine and bring the mixture to a boil.
  3. Reduce the heat to low and let the mixture simmer for about 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the carrots are completely cooked and the mixture has thickened.
  4. Stir in water, cardamom powder, and saffron milk. Cook for another 5-7 minutes, until the halwa has thickened further.
  5. Transfer the halwa to a serving dish and let it cool to room temperature. Once cooled, store it in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

Enjoy your Carrot Halwa!
